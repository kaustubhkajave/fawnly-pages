Fawnly Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Fawnly is a local-first walking and step tracking app. It helps you view your daily movement, walking distance, active calories, floors, daily goals, history, and calm app preferences.

Data Stored on Your Device

Fawnly stores app data locally on your device. This may include:

  • Step history and recent activity history shown in the app
  • Daily walking goal
  • Distance unit preference
  • Theme preference
  • Notification preferences, including the daily reminder and optional Fawnly Plus weekly recap
  • Onboarding and other app settings
  • Legacy height, weight, or stride settings from earlier versions, if already stored on your device

This data is used to make the app work on your device. Fawnly does not sync this data to a Fawnly server.

Accounts and Cloud Sync

Fawnly does not require an account and does not include Fawnly cloud sync. Your activity history and settings stay on your device unless you choose to back up, transfer, or share your device data using operating system features outside of Fawnly.

Ads, Analytics, and Tracking

Fawnly does not include ads or advertising trackers, and does not sell your data. Fawnly does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The only third-party service Fawnly uses is RevenueCat, and only to manage Fawnly Plus subscriptions (see "Purchases and Subscriptions" below).

Apple Health on iPhone

On iPhone, Fawnly can read Apple Health step count, walking and running distance, active energy burned, and flights climbed when you grant permission. Flights climbed may be displayed as floors in Fawnly. This data is processed on your device and is used only to show your activity and history inside the app.

Fawnly does not write data to Apple Health. Fawnly does not transmit Apple Health or activity data to a Fawnly server or to RevenueCat.

You can revoke Fawnly's Apple Health access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Fawnly.

Fawnly does not use Motion & Fitness or Core Motion as an iPhone activity fallback. If Apple Health access is not available, activity totals may not refresh until access is restored.

Android Step Counting

On Android, Fawnly reads your activity from Health Connect when it is available and you grant permission; otherwise it counts live steps using the device step sensor while the app is open. Android distance and calorie values may be estimates. This activity data is used only to show your movement and history inside the app and stays on your device.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. If enabled, Fawnly can schedule a local daily reminder with generic copy. Fawnly Plus subscribers can also enable an optional weekly recap notification that opens the weekly step card. These notifications do not include exact step counts, calories, or distance.

Fawnly requests notification permission only when you choose to enable reminders. You can turn reminders off in Fawnly settings or revoke notification permission in your device settings.

Activity Values

On iPhone, steps, walking and running distance, active calories, and flights climbed come from Apple Health when available. If Apple Health does not provide distance, active calories, or flights climbed for a period, Fawnly shows those values as unavailable rather than calculating replacements.

On Android, distance and calorie values may be estimates and are provided for general wellness context only.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Fawnly offers an optional subscription, Fawnly Plus. Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); Fawnly never sees your card or full payment details.

To unlock Plus features and restore purchases across your devices, Fawnly uses RevenueCat, Inc. as its subscription manager. RevenueCat receives an app-generated, random subscriber ID and your purchase and subscription history, along with basic device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, and country). RevenueCat does not receive your Apple Health or activity data. RevenueCat processes this information to verify and manage your subscription. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Fawnly does not use this purchase information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Not Medical Advice

Fawnly is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have health concerns or questions about physical activity, talk with a qualified healthcare professional.

Deleting Your Data

Because Fawnly stores app data locally on your device, you can delete Fawnly app data by deleting Fawnly from your device or clearing the app's stored data through your device settings where supported.

Apple Health records remain managed by Apple Health because Fawnly only reads them. You can revoke Fawnly's Health access in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Fawnly, and you can revoke notification permission in your device settings.

To ask about subscription-related data associated with Fawnly, including deletion requests where applicable, contact us at fawnlyapp@outlook.com. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may retain transaction records under their own policies and legal obligations.
